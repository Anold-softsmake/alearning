## Coverage and source qualification

The full text of Lesson 1 was read and used as the primary lecturer source for Topic 1. Its scope includes the definition and history of AI, contributing disciplines, types, advantages, disadvantages, challenges, tools and prerequisites. The course outline also explicitly requires goals, approaches and techniques; those are identified in Topic 1 as supplementary coverage.

The other listed lecturer files were identified in the repository, but their full contents were not independently verified during this integration. Their filenames are not treated as proof of detailed topic coverage. Topic pages therefore distinguish known lecturer material from supplementary explanations based on the course outline. The exact label used on each relevant page is: **Missing from the lecturer's notes but required by the course outline.**

Topics 4–9 have been structured from the official course-outline headings and supplemented with standard introductory AI explanations. They should be reconciled against the lecturer's corresponding notes when their full text is available.
